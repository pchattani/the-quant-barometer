Methodology

How the number is made

The Quant Barometer is a research project: statistical estimates that are frequently wrong. Nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation to trade. Data from national statistics offices, the BLS, StatCan, ONS, Eurostat, EIA, the Federal Reserve Banks of Cleveland (CC BY) and Philadelphia, the Atlanta Fed, the New York Fed, BCB, BCRA and the Kalshi public API; each is used under its own terms.
